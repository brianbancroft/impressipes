Impressipes brand (IMP-13)

Impress + recipes. A warm, cute family app for recipes and shopping lists. The pie mascot from IMP-19 is the mark, rhubarb pink from IMP-14 is the accent, Nunito 800 is the wordmark.

  • Name: Impressipes, written impressipes in the wordmark and “Impressipes” in sentence text. Domain impressipes.bancroft.io.
  • Mark: the pie mascot, hello face, leaf at 1 o’clock. Below 48 px it becomes the small mark: no face, a lattice, 12 big scallops.
  • Colour: rhubarb #C62F55 filling and mustard crust on cream #FFF8F0; #FF8DA5 and #CFA52E on #1A1512 in dark. No new colours.
  • App icon: pie on cream for iOS and watchOS; dark and tinted variants for iOS 18. The watch circle crop keeps the whole pie.
  • Web: SVG favicon that follows the colour scheme, ICO and PNG fallbacks, apple-touch-icon, manifest icons, 1200 × 630 OG image.

Personality and voice

Who the app is when it talks. The pie shows up to cheer or console, never to decorate. Copy is short and plain.

Warm

A home kitchen, not a restaurant. Family names, real food, cream paper and brown ink.

“Sam added 3 things to Costco.”

“Your collaborative list has been updated.”

Playful, quietly

The pie appears in empty, done, offline and error states. It is never on a busy screen, never a pattern, never in the way of the list.

“All done. Nothing left to get.”

“Woohoo!! 🎉🎉 You crushed it!!!”

Plain-spoken

Short words, sentence case, verbs on buttons. One exclamation mark at most, and only to celebrate.

“Add to list” · “Try again”

“Proceed” · “An unexpected error has occurred”

Tagline

Recipes to impress.
One shopping list for the family.

Used on the OG image and sign-in. Two lines, the second is the useful one.

Naming in copy

  • “Impressipes”, capital I, in sentences and the App Store.
  • Never “Impressipies”, “ImpressiPes” or “the Impressipes app”.
  • The mascot has no name in the UI. It is “the pie” in docs and alt text.

Construction

  • Crust: mustard, 22 scallops (small: 12).
  • Filling: accent, with a 14% ink rim for depth.
  • Face: on-accent eyes and smile, accent-soft cheeks.
  • Leaf: leaf colour, at 1 o’clock, never moves.

Clear space and minimums

  • Clear space: one filling radius (40 units) on every side.
  • Primary mark minimum 48 px; small mark 16 px.
  • Lockup minimum 120 px wide.

Don’t

  • Recolour outside the tokens, or put the pie on rhubarb (the filling disappears).
  • Rotate, tile, crop or outline it.
  • Change the face in the logo. Other faces belong to empty states only.

Wordmark and lockups

Lowercase impressipes in Nunito 800, ink colour, tracking −1. The pie sits left of the word in the horizontal lockup and above it in the stacked one.

Every file is generated by designs/imp-13/brand.mjs and rendered by render.sh. Change the generator, not the files.

Where they go

  • IMP-27 (XcodeGen): copy the two appiconsets into each target's Assets.xcassets as AppIcon. Both targets already set ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME = AppIcon.
  • IMP-44 (web shell): favicon, icons, manifest and OG tags are already in apps/web. Self-host Nunito 800 there for the wordmark.
  • IMP-30, IMP-31: primary mark above sign-in and invite titles.
